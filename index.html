The shop

A proper barbershop

Jill’s private suite on NE Main Street is where regulars come for the exact cut they ask for—no surprises, just a clean result every time. The space is quiet, unhurried, and built for conversation as much as clippers.

After years away, Jill has returned to Lewistown and reopened her chair. The same steady hand and warm welcome that longtime clients remember are back, now in Suite 2 above the old hardware store.
